Targets: `prd.md`, `addendum.md` (both in this directory)
Date: 2026-09-19

## Summary

The PRD carries the source's *technical* content with unusual fidelity — nearly every milestone, verified measurement and architectural finding in the notes has a home in an FR, a constraint, a non-goal or an addendum entry, and the milestone map in addendum §E makes the two documents genuinely reconcilable. What the FR structure lost is the source's **motivational and rhetorical layer**: the named employer and the specific reasoning about *their* repos, the ranking of which findings are the best interview answers, and the staged plan with its effort estimates and its explicit "do not skip ahead" sequencing discipline. Two smaller categories also thinned out: the failure-mode reasoning behind some delivered fixes (why the naive version would have been worthless), and a handful of concrete artifacts the source had already drafted (the CV bullet, the `appliedRoutes` status field).

## Gaps — content dropped

For a solo builder against a live job search where §1.1 says "timing is load-bearing," this is the most consequential omission: the notes tell you what to do first and roughly how long you have; the PRD does not. Related, also gone: *"they are the reason this project is worth three weeks rather than three days."* *Where it belongs:* a new §8.3 Phasing in the PRD (Stage A = FR-6–FR-11 shipped; Stage B = FR-13–FR-27; Stage C = FR-28–FR-30), and the estimates carried into `bmad-create-epics-and-stories`.

## Distortions — content changed or overstated

- **medium** §5's resource-ceiling NFR asserts *"Anything that does not fit is a non-goal, not a deferral"* as a blanket rule. The source contradicts this twice with explicit deferrals under the same constraint: the permanent cgroup fix is *"Lilian's call and deliberately deferred"* (`kernelCommandLine = cgroup_no_v1=all` in `.wslconfig`, then `wsl --shutdown`), and Calico is *"deferred because this host has 3.7 GB total."* The source treats the 3.7 GB host as a current condition with a known escape hatch; the PRD promotes it to a permanent boundary. The escape hatch does survive in addendum §C, which makes the PRD's absolute phrasing the odd one out.

- **low** FR-10 claims *"A single command brings up a kind cluster running the Gateway, Postgres, and the configuration they need."* The source's M2/M3 Makefile is a sequence of discrete targets — `cluster-up`, `image`, `load`, `deploy` — and nothing in the notes describes a single composite target. Either add one, or restate FR-10 as "a documented Makefile sequence."

- **low** FR-12 is substantially more specific than its source. M5 is one line offering Helm *or* Kustomize; FR-12 asserts the chart installs the Gateway, Postgres **and the Operator**, ships the CRD in `crds/`, exposes image tag / replica count / resync interval as values, and that `helm uninstall` leaves nothing but the PVC. None of that is in the notes. The requirements are sensible, but they are PRD invention presented at the same confidence as delivered work; §11 discloses only the Helm-over-Kustomize choice, not the scope expansion.

- **low** The demo beat's duration drifted. Source M17: *"**That last beat is the whole story in five seconds.**"* FR-30 and SM-1 both say "under ten seconds" — while UJ-2 in the same PRD calls it "the five-second proof." Pick one; the source says five.

- **low** *(from code, not the source — flagged because the PRD asserts it as delivered)* FR-1's consequence list reads as complete but omits a write-ordering asymmetry. `src/services/gateway.rs` `create_vpn_tunnel` calls `self.routing_table.add_route(...)` **before** the Postgres `INSERT` — the reverse of `delete_vpn_tunnel`, whose Postgres-first ordering addendum §D praises precisely because *"the reverse order would produce a pod whose cache disagrees with the durable record until the next hydration."* On a DB failure the create path produces exactly that state. FR-1 says only "A database failure returns `INTERNAL` and the call reports no success." Worth a consequence in FR-1 or a line in addendum §D.
